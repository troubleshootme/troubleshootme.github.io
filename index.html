💰 Quote Discipline: Trade Flexibility for Savings

  1. Offer two service choices instead of one unexplained price.

    • Appointment-protected: Price against accepted, qualified capacity.
    • Flexible: Tie savings to a wider pickup window, faster unloading or a confirmed positioning opportunity.
    • This exposes the customer’s service-cost trade-off without quietly absorbing risk.

  2. Use short validity where density and rate momentum agree.

    • For uncovered Jackson, Phoenix, Salt Lake City, Denver and Boise freight, consider 30–60-minute validity where operationally practical.
    • Refresh after expiration or shipment changes. A valid quote is not a confirmed truck.

  3. Separate fuel from linehaul.

    • Diesel is $5.898/gallon, following 14 consecutive daily declines.
    • Apply contractual fuel-index and reset provisions. Do not count the same relief once in the surcharge and again in linehaul.
    • Falling fuel improves trip economics but does not remove hot-origin costs, empty miles or holiday waiting.

  4. Price complete shipment cost before margin.

    • Include the carrier purchase, known accessorials and shipment-specific recovery exposure.
    • For a gross-margin target: customer price = included cost ÷ (1 − target gross-margin rate).
    • Distinguish gross margin from markup and follow the brokerage’s accounting policy.
