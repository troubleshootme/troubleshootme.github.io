💰 Quote Discipline: Negotiate With Alternatives, Not Anchors

  1. Use shorter validity where density and rates agree.

    • For uncovered Atlanta, Phoenix, Jackson, Denver and Joliet shipments, consider 30–60-minute validity, where practical.
    • Refresh the purchase when the quote expires or shipment specifications change. Quote validity is not confirmed capacity.

  2. Offer a firm-service price and a flexibility price.

    • Tie savings to a wider loading window, fewer stops, faster unloading or a verified positioning benefit.
    • Keep the operating difference visible so the customer understands what produces the savings.

  3. Challenge both sides’ anchoring.

    • A customer’s $2.86/mile national average does not price an urgent Atlanta pickup.
    • A carrier’s historical premium does not establish today’s Peoria purchase.
    • Use qualified alternatives and current acceptance—not arguments about broad market labels.

  4. Keep fuel relief separate from freight leverage.

    • Diesel is $5.984/gallon, with 14 consecutive daily declines. Lower futures support the possibility of further relief, not a guaranteed timing or amount.
    • Apply each contract’s fuel-index reset provisions. Do not double-count the same relief through both fuel recovery and linehaul reductions.

  5. Protect contribution with a complete cost floor.

    • Include the carrier purchase, known shipment costs and appropriate recovery exposure.
    • When pricing to a gross-margin target: customer price = included cost ÷ (1 − target margin). Apply the brokerage’s accounting policy consistently.

Customer language: “Your pickup market supports firmer coverage pricing, but we can test a lower-cost option with more loading flexibility and a carrier that values the destination. The firm option protects the required appointment.”

  • Tightening trigger—offers rise while qualified acceptance weakens: Cover earlier, shorten validity and obtain customer flexibility before adding uncovered commitments.
  • Easing trigger—competitive acceptance improves despite hot density: Capture confirmed savings in Memphis, Little Rock or Houston; do not buy ahead solely because a rebound seems plausible.
  • Disruption trigger—weather holds threaten subsequent appointments: Rebuild the entire truck schedule, including receiving and reload commitments, before promising recovery.

Bottom line: sell verified origin pressure, negotiate destination value, and measure savings only after the truck, equipment and calendar all work.
