  • Easing trigger—competitive acceptance improves despite hot density: Capture confirmed savings; do not buy ahead solely because a rebound seems plausible.
  • Disruption trigger—weather holds threaten the next appointment: Rebuild the truck’s entire schedule, including reloads, before promising recovery.

Bottom line: sell origin certainty, negotiate destination value, and recognize savings only after equipment, route and calendar all work.
