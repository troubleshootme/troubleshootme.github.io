💰 Pricing Discipline: Sell Certainty Without Defending Stale Premiums

Diesel is $5.666/gallon, and fuel relief is already occurring. That does not eliminate equipment, waiting or destination-exit costs.

  • Apply fuel changes once. Follow the contractual index and reset schedule. Do not duplicate a surcharge reduction through an unsupported second deduction from linehaul.
  • Separate protected and flexible service.
    • Protected service requires an accepted carrier commitment, verified appointment feasibility and written accessorial responsibilities.
    • Flexible service offers potential savings in exchange for specific, operationally useful flexibility.
  • Use short quote validity where exposure is real. For uncovered hot-origin spot freight, a 30–60-minute validity window is a practical starting policy, adjusted to customer requirements and live carrier availability. It is a risk-control choice—not a map-derived rate.
  • Reject both sides’ anchoring. A shipper’s national-rate headline and a carrier’s four-day percentage increase are incomplete arguments. Bring the discussion back to today’s accepted truck and complete operating commitment.
  • Keep news subordinate to execution. Institutional purchases of trucking stocks do not establish additional available trucks. No current tender-rejection series is supplied, so do not claim that contract rejection rates are rising or falling.

5. Audit weekend-to-Monday handoffs Confirm facility hours, after-hours contacts, layover exposure and reload timing No discount depends on an unavailable dock or speculative next load.
  • Base operating case—fragmentation persists. Concentrated hot origins coexist with substantial cool-origin buying opportunities. Keep offers market-, equipment- and appointment-specific.
  • Tightening trigger—qualified acceptance deteriorates. If workable offers disappear, cancellations increase or covering times lengthen, secure critical capacity and request customer flexibility.
  • Easing trigger—better bids become executable. Capture savings when qualified carriers accept complete terms; do not preserve a premium solely because an older rate map rose.
  • Disruption trigger—the itinerary stops working. Rebuild safe arrival, facility access and legal driver hours before promising recovery.

Bottom line: Today’s advantage is precision—protect hot-origin service, compete cool-origin purchases, and only pay or credit destination value when the carrier’s next move actually works.
