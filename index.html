  1. Sort uncovered loads by failure consequence and booking deadline.

    • Prioritize temperature-sensitive cargo, production-critical deliveries, permit-dependent moves and rigid appointments.
    • Assign each shipment a latest safe booking time, responsible owner and recovery option.

  2. Get operating facts before another price discussion.

    • Confirm the truck’s actual location, preceding unload, trailer configuration and HOS (Hours of Service).
    • Identify dependencies: washout, uncertain unloading, permit issuance, facility access or an unconfirmed driver assignment.

  3. Purchase the right alternative—not merely the lowest offer.

  4. Set a defensible buying ceiling.

    • Use: Maximum carrier commitment = customer revenue − other direct costs − required contribution − risk allowance.
    • Keep known costs separate from contingent exposure.
    • Escalate when executable cost exceeds the ceiling; do not conceal the shortfall with a speculative reload or hoped-for accessorial recovery.
