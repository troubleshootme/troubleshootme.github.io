📦 Partial Freight: Audit First, Consolidate Second

The largest posted-to-paid mismatch deserves the strongest scope controls—not indiscriminate sales expansion.

  • Identify the service model.

    • Separate terminal-network LTL from shared-truck partial service.
    • For LTL, verify classification, dimensions, weight, applicable tariff or contract terms and accessorials.
    • For partials, confirm usable trailer space, loading sequence, securement and commodity compatibility.

  • Compare complete alternatives.

    • Obtain a dedicated option where feasible.
    • Include added stops, route deviation, handling, detention, redelivery and receiving restrictions.
    • Check whether either shipper requires exclusive use or prohibits co-loading.

  • Reject false economy.

    • A cheaper transportation quote can become the more expensive service if it creates a missed production window, extra handling or cargo exposure.
    • Sell consolidation only when both the economics and service plan survive shipment-level review.
