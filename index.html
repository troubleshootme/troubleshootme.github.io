🧭 Savvy Broker's Playbook

🔑 Executive Signal Summary

  • Treat Saturday as a selective-cover market, not a blanket rate increase. Available loads are 102,294, down 20.1% from 128,021 at the comparable capture, while the national average is $2.89/mi. Weekend posting behavior and freight mix can produce that combination without a shortage of trucks on your lane.
  • Protect the carrier’s whole-trip economics. Diesel at $6.483/gal makes empty pickup miles, refrigeration, waiting, and the next load consequential. A nearby truck with a credible turn may be a better buy than a distant truck advertising a lower linehaul.
  • Sell certainty where it matters. Produce appointments, quarter-end pickups, permit-dependent moves, and weather-exposed deliveries deserve confirmed capacity and explicit exception terms—not a quote built from a national average.

🎯 Today’s Desk Order

  1. Cover exposed commitments first. Call carriers on uncovered temperature-controlled, open-deck, and weather-affected freight. Confirm truck location, equipment, pickup access, appointment, and arrival plan before telling the customer a load is covered.
  2. Quote against a specific truck. Keep the customer bill, carrier buy, fuel treatment, expected gross margin, and potential extras separate. Set a short validity window for weekend-sensitive quotes; recheck Monday freight against Monday pickup-specific offers.
  3. Call trucks nearing delivery. Ask where they actually empty and what destination works next. A workable reload can reduce repositioning friction, but never promise a “backhaul discount” before confirming that reload.
  4. Prospect with an execution proposition. Ask quarter-end shippers which pickups cannot slip. Offer a confirmed appointment and a documented delay plan rather than claiming that every lane is tightening.
