With the replacement deadline set for October 6, 2026, fleets operating these devices must immediately transition to compliant hardware to avoid severe roadside enforcement penalties, including out-of-service orders. 💵 Pricing framework that protects margin today

  • Use a four-bucket quote structure:

    • Linehaul: the lane’s base transport value
    • Fuel: today’s real recovery number at $6.269/gal
    • Dwell: detention, layover, rigid appointment exposure
    • Execution risk: weather, route changes, late tender risk, recovery exposure

  • Match pricing style to haul length:

    • Short-haul freight:
    • Best sold all-in
    • Best covered with local capacity
    • Best margin comes from same-day reload visibility
    • Mid-length freight:
    • Use explicit fuel treatment
    • Tighten detention and reschedule language
    • Long-haul freight:
    • Avoid fixed numbers unless fuel and reroute cost are carved out up front
    • If the shipper refuses risk-sharing, decline bad freight rather than win bad freight

  • Shorten quote life aggressively:

    • Today’s quote is a decaying asset
    • The longer a quote sits, the more likely it becomes a free option for the shipper and a liability for the broker

  • Pay for proximity, not theory:

    • A truck sitting nearby with a believable reload is usually cheaper than a “lower-rate” truck with high empty miles and a fragile appointment plan.
