💰 Quote Construction and Negotiation: Protect Contribution Honestly

Build the customer price from an executable carrier purchase, explicit service exposure and required contribution—not by applying map percentages to a lane.

  • Shorten uncovered quote exposure where both signals tighten. For Memphis, Ontario, El Centro, Charlotte and Wilmington, consider 30–60-minute validity where commercially practical. Refresh the purchase after expiration.
  • Trade operational certainty before demanding another reduction. Ask whether the carrier’s price reflects waiting, destination position, equipment or pickup timing. Authorized appointment flexibility and ready freight may produce better savings than repeated rate pressure.
  • Keep fuel treatment separate. Diesel is $6.064/gallon, with retail declines already under way. Apply contractual index and reset provisions; do not count the same relief in both linehaul and fuel adjustments.
  • Do not mistake posted-versus-paid differences for profit. Evaluate contribution after carrier cost, anticipated accessorials, positioning and recovery exposure.
  • Counter anchoring with alternatives, not arguments. A carrier may remember a higher market; a shipper may cite national softness. Present current, scope-matched choices:
    • “This pickup window has a confirmed purchase.”
