💰 Quote Discipline: Turn Flexibility Into a Priced Choice

  1. Offer a firm appointment option and a flexible option.

    • Firm: Base the customer commitment on qualified carrier acceptance.
    • Flexible: Tie savings to a wider loading window, faster unloading, fewer stops or an accepted positioning opportunity.
    • This makes the customer’s trade-off explicit instead of quietly absorbing service obligations.

  2. Shorten validity where density and rates agree.

    • For uncovered Atlanta, Phoenix, Salt Lake City and Stockton freight, use short validity—such as 30–60 minutes where practical.
    • Refresh the purchase after expiration or changes to shipment specifications.
    • Quote validity is not capacity confirmation.

  3. Separate fuel relief from local capacity pricing.

    • Diesel is $5.929/gallon, following 14 consecutive daily declines.
    • Apply each agreement’s fuel-index and reset provisions. Do not count the same relief in both surcharge and linehaul negotiations.
    • Falling fuel supports cost discussions; it does not remove hot-origin premiums or difficult destination economics.

  4. Price complete shipment cost before applying margin.

    • Include the carrier purchase, known accessorials and shipment-specific recovery exposure.
    • When pricing to a gross-margin target: customer price = included cost ÷ (1 − target gross-margin rate).
    • Use the brokerage’s accounting policy consistently and distinguish gross margin from markup.

Customer language: “The pressure is concentrated at your pickup market. We can protect the required appointment at the firm price, or test savings if you can provide a wider loading window. Fuel adjustments will follow your agreed schedule.”

Carrier language: “Is this destination useful for your next load or home-time plan? Quote the trip on that basis, including any empty repositioning you expect.”
