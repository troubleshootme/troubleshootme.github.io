💰 Quote the Whole Movement—and Protect the Contribution

Maximum carrier buy = customer revenue − other direct shipment costs − required contribution − approved risk reserve.

A reserve is a planning allowance, not an actual expense. Avoid counting fuel or accessorials twice.

  • Do not multiply a national mileage average into a firm quote.

    • Short movements can consume most of a working day through loading and appointments.
    • Equipment requirements, empty positioning and receiver practices can outweigh loaded distance.
    • Use complete trip offers from qualified carriers.

  • Make partial freight pass a total-cost gate.

    • Confirm dimensions, weight, stackability, usable trailer space, commodity compatibility and loading order.
    • Document extra stops, handling, route deviation, detention and redelivery exposure.
    • Distinguish shared-truck partial service from terminal-based LTL (Less Than Truckload); their service and pricing structures differ.

  • Negotiate operating changes before demanding price changes.

    • Test whether approved loading flexibility, faster unloading or reduced empty positioning improves the offer.
    • Accept a saving only after the carrier confirms the revised commitment.
    • Do not trade away service requirements without customer authorization.
