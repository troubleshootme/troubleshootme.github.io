💵 Protect Margin Through Scope and Execution

Diesel at $6.256/gallon keeps empty miles and waiting time commercially important, even with retail relief underway. Do not pre-spend anticipated fuel savings through customer discounts.

  • Separate linehaul from fuel adjustments. Apply the agreed fuel index and reset schedule; negotiate transportation service on its own merits.
  • Compare total carrier compensation. Include positioning, stops, detention terms, equipment preparation and weekend requirements.
  • Use shipment-level contribution. Evaluate customer revenue less carrier cost and expected unrecovered execution costs. Do not rank opportunities using category-average spreads.
  • Resolve accessorial rules before dispatch. Confirm free time, documentation, approval contacts and responsibility for rescheduling or cancellation.
  • Keep payment instructions controlled. Verify the contracting entity and payment assignment; collect delivery and accessorial documentation promptly.

Negotiation example: A carrier requesting more because of an inconvenient reload may accept a better appointment window or a separately confirmed onward shipment. Offer a real operational improvement—not an unsupported promise of “plenty of freight back.”
