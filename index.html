6. Recheck market behavior Track qualified offers, acceptance, time to cover and cancellations Repricing follows actual coverage evidence, not map momentum alone.
  • Base planning case—fragmentation persists. Concentrated Southeast tightness and cool buying alternatives remain the best-supported operating assumption. Keep pricing origin-specific.
  • Tightening case—drivers withdraw faster than freight clears. If qualified acceptance weakens or cancellations increase, secure appointment-critical coverage earlier and request customer flexibility before taking more uncovered freight.
  • Easing case—shipping windows close before trucks leave. If compatible trucks accept better offers, capture confirmed savings. Do not defend a premium solely because the market was previously hot.
  • Disruption case—weather breaks the sequence. Rebuild legal arrival, unloading and the next move before promising recovery. A cheaper replacement that cannot execute is not a recovery.

Bottom line: sell the tight origin, buy the complete trip, and protect the operating window that makes the economics work. Today’s edge is converting neighboring-market imbalances into confirmed service—not applying one holiday markup nationwide.
