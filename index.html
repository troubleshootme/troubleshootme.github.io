💵 Negotiate Total Economics, Not a Headline Rate

Diesel at $6.23/gallon makes empty miles and waiting commercially important even as retail fuel relief continues. Apply surcharge changes through the agreed index and reset schedule—not through anticipated wholesale savings.

  • Make positioning visible. At an illustrative 6 miles per gallon, fuel alone costs approximately $1.04 per total truck mile at today’s diesel price. That excludes wages, equipment, maintenance and other expenses. An unnecessarily long empty approach can overwhelm a small loaded-mile concession.
  • Compare complete carrier offers. Include empty positioning, stops, trailer preparation, detention terms, permits and weekend requirements.
  • Calculate shipment contribution. Start with customer revenue, subtract carrier expense and expected unrecovered execution costs. An attractive gross spread can disappear after a missed appointment or unpriced handling requirement.
  • Control quote validity. Tie validity to confirmed capacity and clearly stated shipment assumptions. Shorten it when local offers are changing or equipment requirements remain unresolved.
  • Use operational concessions before price concessions. A confirmed loading window, faster unloading or reduced empty mileage may improve carrier economics without reducing broker contribution.

Negotiation example: If a carrier’s objection is an uncertain Friday loading time, another rate request may not solve it. Secure a reliable loading slot and receiving confirmation, then ask the carrier to reprice the reduced uncertainty.

Avoid anchoring mistakes: Customers may anchor to the national average; carriers may anchor to their best recent load. Bring both discussions back to this shipment’s route, equipment, timing and execution burden.
