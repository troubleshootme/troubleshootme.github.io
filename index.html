⏱️ First 90 Minutes: Turn Conditional Coverage Into Executable Coverage

  1. Rank every open shipment by failure consequence and replacement difficulty.

    • Put temperature-sensitive freight, scarce equipment, permit-dependent moves and fixed receiving appointments first.
    • Identify the latest safe booking and departure times.
    • Assign one owner to each unresolved dependency.

  2. Reconfirm the truck—not merely the carrier’s acceptance.

    • Verify equipment location, preceding delivery, trailer suitability and HOS (Hours of Service).
    • Ask when the truck becomes available, not just when the carrier expects it to arrive.
    • Confirm an authorized dispatch contact and a functioning after-hours escalation path.

  3. Remove avoidable uncertainty before requesting a concession.

    • Obtain actual loading readiness, expected loading duration and destination acceptance.
    • Separate confirmed facts from estimates in the carrier offer.
    • Where customer-approved flexibility produces a better executable purchase, quote it as a distinct service option.

  4. Set a stop-shopping rule.

    • Continue comparing while qualified alternatives remain and departure is protected.
    • Stop when incremental shopping threatens the only feasible truck or the last safe dispatch window.
    • Escalate purchases below the shipment’s contribution floor before booking.

  5. Maintain identity and payment controls under weekend pressure.

    • Verify authority, insurance, contracting entity and pickup authorization through established procedures.
    • Independently verify changes to carrier identity, banking or pickup instructions.
    • Do not let an urgent load or unusually attractive offer bypass qualification.
