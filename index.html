💰 Pricing Discipline: Sell Choices, Not a National Headline

Current reporting shows spot strength without a corresponding increase in tender rejections. The OTRI (Outbound Tender Rejection Index) trend therefore does not support a nationwide contract-capacity shortage pitch. Sell the specific shipment constraint instead.

  1. Use short validity where the signals align.

    • For uncovered Memphis, Birmingham, Allentown, Denver or Las Vegas freight, consider 30–60-minute validity when commercially practical.
    • Refresh carrier acceptance after expiration or a change in pickup requirements.

  2. Offer a firm-service option and an authorized-flexibility option.

    • Price the required pickup separately from a wider appointment window.
    • Identify whether savings come from less waiting, a better truck position or a compatible next load—not an unsupported promise that prices will fall.

  3. Set the customer price from complete cost and required contribution.

    • Include the carrier purchase and known shipment-specific costs before approving the sale.
    • If using a gross-margin target, distinguish it from markup: customer price = included cost ÷ (1 − target margin). Follow the brokerage’s accounting policy.

  4. Avoid emotional anchoring on both sides.

    • A carrier’s remembered peak rate does not establish today’s buy.
    • A customer’s reference to a cool national market does not establish cheap capacity for a tight appointment in Memphis.
    • Present executable alternatives rather than debating whose headline is right.

  5. Keep fuel treatment explicit.

    • Diesel is $6.019/gallon, after 14 consecutive daily declines.
    • Apply each agreement’s fuel index and reset schedule. Do not count the same relief twice through linehaul and surcharge reductions.
    • Wholesale firmness warrants monitoring, but does not justify promising a specific retail-fuel path.

Customer language: “Your origin is tightening even though nearby markets are softer. This price protects the required pickup; the alternative tests savings with your approved scheduling flexibility.”
